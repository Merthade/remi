Reminder or Alarm? When an iPhone Reminder Isn't Enough

You set a reminder to take your tablet at 8. The phone was on Silent. It buzzed once, face down on the sofa, and that was that.

Quick answer: A reminder is a notification: it plays once at notification volume, stays quiet on Silent and in Focus modes, and is easy to dismiss. An alarm rings until you stop it. Use reminders for things that can wait an hour; use an alarm for wake-ups, medication and anything you cannot miss.

What a reminder does

A reminder, in the built-in Reminders app, in Remi, or in any to-do app, arrives as a notification. On iPhone that means:

  • One sound or buzz, at notification volume.
  • No sound when the phone is on Silent, and held back by Focus modes unless you allow the app.
  • Gone the moment you swipe it away.

That is exactly right for most things: buy stamps, call the garage, pay rent this week. You see it when you look at your phone, and nothing breaks if that is an hour later.

What an alarm does

An alarm keeps ringing until you stop or snooze it, and an alarm app built on Apple's alarm system rings even with the phone on Silent. It is not something you can miss by having your phone face down.

Which to use

  • Reminder: errands, chores, bills, renewals, birthdays, ideas, anything with a loose time. Remi is built for these, including the ones with no exact date.
  • Alarm: waking up, medication, catching a flight, leaving for the school run, anything where "an hour later" is a problem.

Plenty of people use both: Remi for the long list of things that need doing sometime, an alarm for the few that need doing now.

Need it to actually ring?

A reminder is a notification: it plays once at notification volume and stays quiet when your phone is on Silent or in a Focus. For a wake-up, a medication dose or anything you cannot miss, use an alarm instead. Questions

Do iPhone reminders make a sound on Silent?

No. Reminders arrive as notifications, which are silent when the phone is on Silent and can be held back by Focus modes. Alarms are different and ring through Silent.

Should I use a reminder or an alarm for medication?

If missing the time matters, use an alarm, because it keeps ringing and is not silenced by the Silent switch. Use a reminder for things that can wait.
